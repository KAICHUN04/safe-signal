# Safe Signal
### Spot the signs. Stay safe online.

Safe Signal is an interactive cybersecurity awareness website designed to educate teenagers (ages 13–18) about online predators, grooming behaviors, digital privacy, and safe online practices through engaging educational content and gamified scenario-based learning.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

In today’s digital world, teenagers are increasingly exposed to online risks such as grooming, manipulation, phishing attempts, and privacy exploitation. Safe Signal aims to help users recognize suspicious online behaviors, understand the importance of digital safety, and make informed decisions while interacting online.

The platform combines:
- interactive learning content,
- realistic chat-based scenarios,
- gamified quizzes,
- and actionable safety guidance

to create a modern and engaging cybersecurity awareness experience.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Educate teenagers about online grooming tactics and predatory behaviors
- Promote safe online habits and digital privacy awareness
- Help users identify suspicious online interactions and red flags
- Encourage users to seek help from trusted adults when necessary
- Deliver cybersecurity awareness through an engaging and interactive platform

---

# 🖥️ Features

## 🏠 Home Dashboard
- Modern responsive landing page
- Interactive call-to-action sections
- Cybersecurity awareness branding

---

## 📖 Decode Zone
Educational content covering:
- Grooming tactics
- Online predators
- Digital footprints
- Privacy awareness
- Safe online behavior

---

## 🚨 Signal Alerts
Interactive red flag examples using realistic chat-style interfaces:
- Requests for selfies
- Asking for personal information
- Emotional manipulation
- Requests to keep secrets
- Moving conversations to private platforms

---

## 🎮 Signal Check
Gamified scenario-based quiz system featuring:
- Realistic online chat simulations
- Multiple-choice decision making
- Score tracking
- Immediate feedback and explanations
- Progress indicators
- Safety evaluation

---

## 📊 Signal Report
Results and performance analysis:
- Final score display
- Safety rating
- Strengths and improvement advice
- Achievement badges

---

## 🛡️ Signal Shield
Safety and support resources:
- Steps to handle suspicious interactions
- Reporting and blocking guidance
- Encouragement to contact trusted adults
- Support message templates
