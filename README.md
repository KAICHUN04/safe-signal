# Safe Signal
### Spot the signs. Stay safe online.

Safe Signal is an interactive cybersecurity awareness website designed to educate teenagers (ages 13–18) about online predators, grooming behaviors, digital privacy, and safe online practices through engaging educational content and gamified scenario-based learning.
https://safe-signal-iota.vercel.app/

---

# 📌 Project Overview

In today’s digital world, teenagers are increasingly exposed to online risks such as grooming, manipulation, phishing attempts, and privacy exploitation. Safe Signal aims to help users recognize suspicious online behaviors, understand the importance of digital safety, and make informed decisions while interacting online.

The platform combines:
- interactive learning content,
- realistic chat-based scenarios,
- gamified quizzes,
- and actionable safety guidance

to create a modern and engaging cybersecurity awareness experience.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Educate teenagers about online grooming tactics and predatory behaviors
- Promote safe online habits and digital privacy awareness
- Help users identify suspicious online interactions and red flags
- Encourage users to seek help from trusted adults when necessary
- Deliver cybersecurity awareness through an engaging and interactive platform

---

# 🖥️ Features

🏠 Home Dashboard
* Modern responsive landing page engineered with a dark mode dashboard layout.
* Clear visual gateways directing target users (teenagers) into educational zones.

📖 Decode Zone
* High-level analytical overview breaking down the exact mechanics of digital manipulation.
* Teaches tactical safety structures through immediate, scannable documentation.

🚨 Signal Alerts
* Interactive hazard dashboard mapping out real-world threat indicators.
* Displays localized examples featuring micro-chat snippets and custom threat level badges (e.g., "Grooming Behavior", "Privacy Risk") to expose online manipulation tactics.

🎮 Signal Check
* A gamified, scenario-based cybersecurity quiz simulator.
* Puts users directly inside realistic online chat interfaces where their conversational decisions dictate their safety score.

🛡️ Signal Shield
* An actionable safety framework built to empower young users to take control.
* Instructs teens on exact defensive sequences: Stop ➔ Save ➔ Block ➔ Tell.

🔗 Resources
* A verified directory mapping local, structural support networks within Malaysia.
* Provides direct contact access, hotlines, and web reporting portals for emergency channels including Talian Kasih, Cyber999, MCMC, and PDRM.
